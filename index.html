THE OPPORTUNITY

West Africa has a blue-carbon opportunity the world is only beginning to price.

Across West Africa, mangroves, tidal wetlands and other coastal ecosystems represent significant natural capital. They capture and store carbon, sustain biodiversity, strengthen coastal resilience and support millions of livelihoods.

Yet much of this value remains outside formal financial and investment systems.

The challenge is not simply the absence of natural assets. It is the absence of the infrastructure required to identify, secure, measure, validate, structure and finance them at scale.

WABC is building that infrastructure.

We develop the institutional, scientific, technical and investment architecture required to translate coastal natural assets into credible, financeable climate and nature projects.
